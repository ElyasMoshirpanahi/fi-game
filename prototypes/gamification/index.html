Gamification prototypes

Throwaway pages, not the game. Each batch asks one question and answers it three or four different ways. Open a batch, play the phone on the left, use the buttons beside it to skip days, and switch variants with the white bar or the arrow keys. Nothing is saved: a reload starts over. Tell me, per batch, which variant feels right, or which parts of two you would combine.

Evidence for each variant is printed beside the phone. The full note is docs/research/gamification-mechanics.md. It advises against hearts, random chests and selling hints; those are here so you can feel them, not because they are recommended.
