# Sizing by value and complexity (proposal for owner approval, 2026-11-10)

Value (decision 31) sets the number of Circles: high 3, mid 2, low 1. Complexity (1 to 5, how dense and technical the book's ideas are) sets the Lessons per Circle and the Points per Lesson. Parkinson: a denser book gets more, longer Lessons; a light book is not padded.

| 2 | 3 | 11 to 12 |
| 3 | 4 | 12 |
| 4 | 4 to 5 | 13 to 14 |
| 5 | 5 | 14 to 15 |
